1. Privacy Policy & Confidentiality

How we safeguard your identity, conversations, and emotional wellbeing.

1.1 Guest Access

You can use guest chat without an account. The service still processes technical request data, including an IP address, for security and rate limits.

1.2 Security & AI Processing

HTTPS protects traffic to our VPS. Saved chat and contact text is encrypted in the database. Chat messages are sent to an external AI service to generate replies. We do not sell them to advertisers.

1.3 Ephemeral Self-Care Tools

Mindfulness exercises, 4-7-8 breathing sessions, and daily mood check-ins are processed ephemerally on your device. We do not construct persistent psychiatric psychological profiles without your explicit instruction.

1.4 Clearing Saved Chats

Signed-in users can clear saved chat history and related doctor access codes. Guest chats remain in the browser session. Contact us about other data requests.
